# Product

## What is Campaniles

Campaniles is an independent, community-first media platform for computer science and information technology. It gives the CS/IT community a place to publish, read, and discuss technical writing, news, and analysis without the noise and incentives of ad-driven feeds.

It is a free and open-source project released under the Apache License 2.0. Contributions, ideas, and feedback from the community are welcome.

## Principles

- **Community first** — the people who read and write here shape the platform.
- **Independent** — no corporate gatekeeping, no pay-to-play ranking.
- **Open source** — the code is public, auditable, and built in the open.
- **Technically focused** — content is for and by the CS/IT community.

## Audience

Software engineers, students, researchers, and technologists who want substantive technical content and discussion.

## Decision guidance

When weighing product and design choices, favor the reader and writer experience over engagement-maximizing patterns. Keep the platform transparent, accessible, and free of dark patterns. Avoid anything that resembles ad-driven ranking or pay-to-play visibility.
